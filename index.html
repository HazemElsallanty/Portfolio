About me

Where I'm coming from and what I'm working towards.

I'm an IT student at the Faculty of Computers and Artificial Intelligence, Damietta University, and an aspiring data engineer. Most of what I know comes from building things end to end: writing the SQL, moving the data, modelling it, and then checking that the numbers hold up.

Through the Digital Egypt Pioneers Initiative I completed the Microsoft Data Engineer track under the Ministry of Communications and Information Technology, which pushed me deeper into Python, database management, ETL processes, data warehousing, big data concepts and cloud data services on Microsoft Azure.

What interests me most is the unglamorous part — scalable pipelines, clean dimensional models, and data quality checks that catch problems before a dashboard does. My goal is to keep growing into a data engineer who can own a warehouse from source files to the reporting layer.
